01 / OPERATIONS

Operational Influence

Once introduced into the enterprise operational environment, information directly informs and influences downstream decisions, actions, operations, and outcomes.

02 / READINESS

Unready Information

Organizations lack an operational mechanism to ready upstream information before that information is acted upon or shapes and influences downstream business operations.

03 / CONSEQUENCE

Consequential Reliance

When unreadied, deficient information reaches or informs a consequential decision; the failure can become operational, regulatory, evidentiary, financial, or human.
